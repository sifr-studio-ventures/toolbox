### Takeaways

- **Team at $490 is about 30% cheaper than the lean stack ($693) and about 72% cheaper than the mid-tier stack ($1,747)** for 10 people, with one login and connected data.
- **At 5 people the gap shrinks.** Team costs $245 against $448 for the lean stack. Small teams can piece together free tiers (PostHog, Tally, Linear Free, Trello Free, Miro Free), so our Free tier must be useful or they will.
- **Per-seat bundling penalises seats that only need one tool.** A 30-person company where only 3 people use CRM pays $49 × 30 for Team. Consider viewer or guest seats (Jam, Coda and Miro do versions of this) or per-tool seats on Team.
- **Competitors have usage fees, and we can choose not to.** Intercom's Fin, Featurebase AI, PostHog events and MAU-metered onboarding tools all scale with usage. "No usage surprises" is a credible message if our infrastructure costs on Cloudflare allow it.
