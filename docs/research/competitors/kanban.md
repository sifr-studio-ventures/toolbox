## Feature notes

- **Linear** is the benchmark for product and engineering teams: fast keyboard-first UI, cycles, projects, roadmaps, triage, customer requests and Git integration. Since 2026 it also has a Linear Agent and Skills on all plans. Startups in its partner programme get Business free, and non-profits get 75% off.
- **Jira** is deep workflow configuration for larger engineering orgs. It's usually bought together with Confluence; Jira + Confluence Standard for 50 users is about $666/month.
- **Trello** is the simplest kanban, with Power-Ups and Butler automation. It's cheap, but light on product-specific workflow.
- **ClickUp** is everything-in-one: tasks, docs, whiteboards, chat, goals, forms. It's the closest generalist to our breadth on the work-management side, but has no support chat, product analytics or bug capture.
- **Asana / monday.com** are cross-functional work management with strong portfolio and reporting views, less tuned to product teams.

## Implications for Open Toolbox

- Our kanban competes most directly with **Linear Basic ($10) and Trello Standard ($5)**. To be credible, a board needs at minimum: drag-and-drop columns, card detail (the design system's modal pattern), assignees, labels, due dates (the native date field) and keyboard shortcuts.
- The differentiator is the **inputs**: surveys, bug reports, support tickets and CRM requests that become cards automatically. Linear charges for this ("Customer Requests", integrations); we can make it native.
- Watch Linear's startup programme (free Business). Early-stage teams are our target too.
