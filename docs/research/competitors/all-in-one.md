## Read-across

- **The work-management suites** (ClickUp, Notion, monday, Atlassian) cover the *internal* half: kanban, docs, canvas. None covers the *customer-facing* half: analytics, in-app popups, surveys, support chat.
- **The customer-facing suites** (PostHog, Featurebase, HubSpot) cover the *external* half, with no real kanban or docs.
- **Open Toolbox is the only pitch that joins both halves.** The risk is depth: each half has a strong specialist.

## Most dangerous competitors

1. **PostHog** — developer-loved, near-free for small teams, and steadily adding products (surveys, error tracking, data warehouse, LLM analytics, workflows). If it adds a board, it eats our analytics, surveys and bugs story.
2. **Featurebase** — feedback → roadmap → changelog → support → surveys at $29/seat. It's the closest to our "customer signal into the backlog" loop.
3. **ClickUp** — the broadest "replace everything" brand, with forms and whiteboards. Its AI upsell ($9–28/user) erodes its price advantage.
4. **Notion** — the default doc and wiki tool for startups. Teams tend to keep Notion alongside anything else they adopt.
