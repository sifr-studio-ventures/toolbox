# CRM

Our pitch: *Keep accounts, contacts, and pipeline context beside product decisions.* (Team tier: "Shared CRM + support chat")

## Feature notes

- **HubSpot** is the full platform: marketing, sales, service, CMS and ops hubs. It's cheap to start, but the jump to Professional is steep, with a large onboarding fee.
- **Attio** is the modern, data-model-first CRM that's popular with startups. AI credits are metered per seat and per workspace. It is our closest spiritual competitor for a product-minded CRM.
- **Pipedrive** is pipeline-first and easy for small sales teams.
- **folk** is a lightweight relationship CRM.

## Implications for Open Toolbox

- CRM is placed in **Team ($49)**. That beats Attio Plus ($35) **plus** the other nine tools, but CRM-heavy buyers will compare depth against Attio.
- Our angle is **product context**: an account shows its support conversations, feature requests (cards), survey answers and product usage (analytics). Attio and HubSpot charge extra for, or need integrations to get, product usage data.
- Keep v1 simple: accounts, contacts, deals, pipeline board (reuse the kanban), notes, and an activity timeline.
