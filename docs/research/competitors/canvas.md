# Canvas / whiteboard

Our pitch: *Map journeys, flows, and product bets on a shared visual surface.*

## Pricing (USD per member / month, annual billing)

| Tool | Free | Entry paid | Mid tier | Top tier | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Miro** | 3 editable boards, unlimited members, 10 AI credits | Starter **$8** ($10 monthly), 25 AI credits/member | Business **$20** ($25 monthly), SSO, guests | Enterprise custom, 30-member minimum | 3rd-party, consistent across sources verified Aug–Oct 2026 |
| **FigJam** | Free (Figma Starter) | No longer sold on its own: included with paid Figma seats. Collab seat ~**$5** (Organization) | Figma Professional ~$12–16 per full seat | Organization ~$45–55 | 3rd-party, conflicting |
| **Whimsical** | 3 boards | ~**$10** / editor | — | — | 3rd-party, single source |

## Feature notes

- **Miro** is the category leader: templates, sticky notes, diagramming, workshops, AI clustering and Jira/Asana sync. One gotcha: Starter has no guest role, so client collaboration forces Business at $20.
- **FigJam** is free or cheap for teams already on Figma. It has the strongest design-team pull.
- **Whimsical** combines flowcharts, wireframes, mind maps and docs, close to our "journeys, flows" pitch.

## Implications for Open Toolbox

- A full Miro-class canvas is a big build. Scope v1 to **journey maps and flows** (boxes, connectors, sticky notes, frames) that link to cards and docs, in Whimsical's lane rather than Miro's.
- Price anchor is $8–10 per editor. Giving free viewer and guest access would beat Miro Starter's missing guest role.
