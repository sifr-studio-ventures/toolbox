## Feature notes

- **Intercom** is the premium messenger, inbox, help center and Fin AI agent. Fin is the main cost driver: a two-seat Advanced team at 500 resolutions a month pays about $665/month.
- **Zendesk** is the enterprise ticketing standard. Live chat only comes with the Suite tiers.
- **Help Scout** is a shared inbox with a human feel; its flat pricing is SMB-friendly.
- **Crisp** prices per workspace, which is the cheapest route for small teams: 4 agents for $45.
- **Featurebase** combines support inbox, live chat and help center with feedback boards, roadmap, changelog and surveys. It is the closest product to our "support + feedback + backlog" story.

## Implications for Open Toolbox

- AI resolution fees ($0.49–$0.99) are now standard. If we add an AI agent, decide between metering it and bundling a quota per seat.
- Crisp's per-workspace pricing ($45 for 4 agents) is the floor that SMBs compare against. Our Team tier at $49/seat costs more per support agent, so we justify it with **everything else included**.
- The killer integration is a ticket that becomes a card, plus a CRM account timeline. Featurebase does feedback-to-roadmap, but has no kanban, CRM or analytics.
