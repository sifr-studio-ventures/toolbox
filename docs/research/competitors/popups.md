# Popups & in-app prompts

Our pitch: *On-site prompts for announcements, upgrades, and guided moments.*

The word "popups" spans two markets with very different prices. Our copy mentions announcements, upgrades and guided moments, which describes **in-app messaging** for a product team rather than lead-gen popups.

## A. Marketing-site popups (lead capture)

| Tool | Model | Free | Entry | Mid | Top | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **OptinMonster** | impressions, annual | — | Basic **$7/mo** intro (renews ~$210/yr) | Plus ~$19, Pro **$29** intro (renews ~$870/yr; exit-intent starts here) | Growth ~$49 | 3rd-party, conflicting |
| **Poptin** | visitors | **1,000 visitors/mo**, unlimited popups | Basic **$25** (10K visitors) | Pro **$59** (50K) | Agency **$119** (150K) | 3rd-party |

## B. In-app onboarding & messaging (product teams)

| Tool | Meter | Entry | Higher tiers | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Userpilot** | MAU | Starter **~$249–299/mo** (~2,000 MAU, annual) | Growth / Enterprise custom | 3rd-party (several written by competitors) |
| **Appcues** | MAU | Spark **~$3,600/yr** (≤1,000 MAU) | Start / Grow / Enterprise custom | 3rd-party |
| **Chameleon** | MTU | Startup **$279/mo** (2,000 MTU) | Pro from **$750/mo** (5K MTU), Growth from **$1,250/mo** | 3rd-party (vendor's own comparison) |

## Implications for Open Toolbox

- In-app prompt tools start around **$250–300/month**. That is the single most expensive line item a small product team replaces, and our Pro tier includes popups at $19/seat. It's our strongest pricing story.
- Build v1 as **in-app**: announcement banners, modals (reusing the modal pattern), tooltips, targeted by analytics segments, with survey and form embeds.
- Avoid metering by MAU early. Competitors' MAU and MTU definitions confuse buyers, and flat pricing is a selling point.
