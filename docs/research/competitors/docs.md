## Feature notes

- **Notion** is the default for startups: docs, wikis, databases, projects, a calendar and AI. In May 2025 it folded full AI into Business and ended the separate AI add-on for Plus. In 2026 it added Custom Agents (credit-billed) and Workers. It's priced per member, so every editor pays.
- **Confluence** is the enterprise wiki, strongest when paired with Jira.
- **ClickUp Docs** are bundled into the work tool, which shows how a docs-plus-tasks bundle gets sold.
- **Coda** charges only for the people who make docs, not those who edit them. That's a pricing idea worth noting.

## Implications for Open Toolbox

- Docs is table stakes inside a bundle; nobody switches for docs alone. The win is **docs linked to board cards, CRM accounts and survey results**.
- Notion Plus at $10/seat is the price anchor. Our Pro tier at $19 includes docs, so it has to feel at least as good for specs and PRDs: slash commands, embeds, mentions of cards and accounts, and templates (PRD, launch note).
- Consider Coda-style free viewers and commenters. That matches Jam's free viewer seats and lowers friction when adopting a bundle.
