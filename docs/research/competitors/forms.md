# Forms

Our pitch: *Capture intake, waitlists, and requests without a separate form stack.*

## Feature notes

- **Tally** has a doc-style builder (type `/` to add blocks), logic, payments, webhooks, and Notion/Airtable integrations. Its free plan resets the market's expectations.
- **Typeform** offers one-question-at-a-time conversational forms with strong design. Response caps are its pain point.
- **Jotform** has the broadest templates, PDFs, payments, HIPAA and approvals.

## Implications for Open Toolbox

- Tally makes forms effectively free, so **forms can't be a paid wedge on their own**. Include generous forms on our Free tier.
- Our value is where submissions go: **a form response becomes a CRM contact (waitlist/intake) or a kanban card (request)**. Neither Tally nor Typeform does this natively; they rely on Zapier and webhooks.
- Reuse the design system's field patterns (native select, native date, Active focus border) so embedded forms look like the product.
