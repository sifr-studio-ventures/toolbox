## Key takeaways

1. **No one bundles all ten tools.** The nearest suites each cover three to five of them: PostHog (analytics, surveys, session replay, feature flags), Featurebase (feedback, support inbox, surveys, changelog), and Notion or ClickUp (docs, kanban, light CRM). Product, sales and support stay split across separate vendors. That split is our opening.
2. **Assembling the stack separately costs about $700/month for a lean 10-person team, and about $1,700/month on mainstream mid-tier tools** (see [pricing-comparison.md](pricing-comparison.md)). Open Toolbox's planned Pro tier ($19/seat) comes to $190/month and Team ($49/seat) to $490/month for 10 seats. Both are well under the mid-tier stack.
3. **Prices are moving to usage and AI fees.** Intercom charges $0.99 per Fin resolution, Featurebase $0.49 per AI resolution, ClickUp sells Brain AI at $9/user, Attio meters AI credits, Notion bills Custom Agents in credits, and PostHog is fully per-unit. A flat per-seat bundle is a clear contrast. Any AI we add needs a pricing decision up front.
4. **Generous free tiers set expectations.** Tally (unlimited forms), PostHog (1M events), Linear, Trello, Miro, Notion and Jam all have real free plans. Our Free tier has to be useful on its own, not just a sign-in page.
5. **The quality bar per category is high.** Linear sets it for kanban, Notion for docs, Miro for canvas, and Tally for forms. As a bundle we can win on integration (one login, one org, shared customer and board data) before we match each specialist on depth.

## Method and confidence

- Live pricing pages couldn't be fetched from the research environment. Prices were checked with searches restricted to vendor domains where possible, marked **(official)**. The rest come from 2026 third-party pricing guides, marked **(3rd-party)**, and were cross-checked across at least two sources. Where sources disagreed, the files say so.
- All prices are USD per month on annual billing, unless noted.
- Vendors reprice often. Attio changed its prices in July 2026, Featurebase its AI fee between May and July 2026, and Typeform and Intercom both run promotions. Re-check before using a figure externally.
